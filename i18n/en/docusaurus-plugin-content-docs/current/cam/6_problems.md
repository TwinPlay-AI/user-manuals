# Troubleshooting

## ![fast_blink](/img/blink1_200ms_50_0ms.gif) The indicator light blinks rapidly

| Issue | Solutions |
|----------|-----------|
| Recording is paused | Press the button briefly to resume recording |

## ![single_blink](/img/blink1_single_short.gif) The indicator light gives a single short blink

The camera is not detected: the unit is waiting to reconnect.

| Issue | Solutions |
|----------|-----------|
| The unit does not detect the camera | Verify that the cable is connected to one of the two USB ports on the unit.<br/>Check that the USB cable is connected to the camera.<br/>Restart the unit. |

## No WiFi network available

The camera no longer enters a dedicated pairing mode: if no known network is found it simply records offline. Configure WiFi networks from the mobile app (see [WiFi Configuration with Mobile App](3_configuration.md)). If uploads are not happening, check that the camera has a working network with internet access.
